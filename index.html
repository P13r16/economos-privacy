Economos Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Economos collects nothing.

There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Your subscriptions never leave your device, and the operating system enforces that rather than Economos merely promising it.

The short version

Economos is an offline subscription tracker. Everything you enter is stored in private app storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded, because the app is not permitted to reach the internet at all.

Economos has no internet permission

The Android version of Economos does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission. This is the single most important fact on this page, so it is worth being precise about what it means.

An Android app cannot open a network connection without that permission. It is not a setting Economos can respect or ignore; the operating system denies the capability outright. Any attempt to send your data anywhere would fail at the system level.

The only permissions Economos requests are:

PermissionWhy
POST_NOTIFICATIONSSo a renewal reminder can appear. You choose whether to grant it.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDSo scheduled reminders survive a restart of your phone.
WAKE_LOCKSo a reminder can be delivered reliably when the phone is idle.

There is no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or network permission. Economos cannot read your files, and it cannot see your other apps.

Where your data is stored

Your subscriptions, notes and choices are stored in the app's private storage on your device. That storage is readable only by Economos. It is not on the SD card, not in shared storage, and not visible to other apps.

Your language and currency preferences are stored separately, in the same private area. So is your light or dark theme choice.

If you export or back up your data yourself, that copy is wherever you put it. Economos uses the standard Android system share sheet or file picker for that, so you choose the destination. The optional encrypted backup feature is encrypted on your device before it is written.

Your data is not included in device backups

Economos sets android:allowBackup="false" and ships explicit backup rules. Your subscription data is therefore not copied into Google cloud backup, and is not included in device-to-device transfer.

This is a deliberate privacy decision. The tradeoff is that uninstalling Economos deletes your data permanently, because the only copy lived on that device. Use the export feature if you want to keep a copy somewhere else.

Reminders and notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device using the Android alarm system. They are computed locally from the renewal dates you entered. No server is contacted to schedule them, and no server is contacted when one fires.

Notifications are generated entirely on your device. Economos does not use a push service, and there is no push token, because there is nowhere to send one.

No analytics, no crash reporting, no third parties

Economos contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting service, no A/B testing framework and no social or contact widgets. There are no third-party network requests of any kind, because the app cannot make them.

The app is self-contained: its fonts, code and artwork all ship inside the APK. It does not load anything from a content delivery network or a web font service at runtime.

In-app purchases

The current release of Economos contains no purchases and no paid features. Every feature is available in full, at no cost, to everyone.

If a paid option is offered in a future release, it will be processed by Google Play. Google handles the payment; Economos never receives or stores your card details, and the transaction data would be disclosed in this policy and in the Google Play Data safety form at that time.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything from inside the app: the Privacy panel has a Clear all data control that asks for confirmation first.

Deleting individual subscriptions works from each card. Uninstalling Economos removes all of its private storage, including anything not deleted explicitly.

Children

Economos is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because Economos collects nothing, there is no personal data held about you for us to disclose, correct, export or delete. Everything you have entered is already in your hands, on your device, and you can export it at any time.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Economos changes what the app does, this policy will be updated to match before or alongside that release. The current version number of the app is always available on its Google Play listing.
